## Notes

Research note: Wikipedia's article is the only source. It also names Demis Roussos and Weezer without releases, and it lists Kenny Lynch (1964 UK single), Muhammad Ali (1964 single), Mickey Gilley, and others I left out as novelty or low fit. The song descends from the Soul Stirrers' "Stand by Me Father" (Sam Cooke and J. W. Alexander) and the hymn "Stand by Me" by Charles Albert Tindley. John owns no version, so the page has no holdings.
