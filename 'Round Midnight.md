# 'Round Midnight

## Composition Info
- Composer: Thelonious Monk (1944), with intro/bridge often credited to Cootie Williams and Dizzy Gillespie
- Monk's most famous composition, most recorded jazz standard after "Summertime"
- Original lyrics by Bernie Hanighen
- Minor key, melancholic, became the ultimate late-night closer
- Ted Gioia: "The national anthem of jazz" (p. XXX)

## Why This Standard Matters
This is **THE test piece** - if you're a jazz musician, you play 'Round Midnight. Having 15+ versions in collection isn't completism, it's documentation of how universal this composition became.

## Listening Project: Comparative Analysis

With 15+ versions, you can trace:

### Evolution of interpretation (chronological):
1. **Monk originals** (1940s-50s) - composer's intent
2. **Miles studio** (1957 - missing, should acquire)
3. **Gil Evans orchestral** (1958)
4. **Miles Paris live** (1960)
5. **Blakey Tokyo** (1961)
6. **Evans Penthouse** (1963)
7. **Miles Plugged Nickel** (1965) - deconstruction
8. **Dexter Homecoming** (1976)
9. **Makaya remix** (2024) - 80 years later

### By instrument/approach:
- **Piano solo**: Monk Pure Monk, Evans
- **Piano trio**: Evans Penthouse, MJQ
- **Trumpet**: Miles (2 versions), Gillespie
- **Saxophone**: Dexter, Illinois Jacquet
- **Vocals**: Sarah Vaughan (2 versions)
- **Guitar solo**: Joe Pass
- **Organ**: Jimmy McGriff
- **Orchestral**: Gil Evans
- **Drums/beat**: Makaya McCraven
- **Ensemble**: Monk Genius, Blakey

### Specific comparisons to make:
1. **Monk solo vs. Monk ensemble** - What does accompaniment add/subtract?
2. **Miles 1960 vs. Miles 1965** - Straight-ahead → avant-garde in 5 years
3. **MJQ early vs. MJQ late** - Did the chamber jazz approach evolve?
4. **Sarah Vaughan 1975 vs. Japan** - Same arrangement or different?
5. **Joe Pass unaccompanied** - Does the composition hold up with no rhythm section?

## Collection Strategy

**This is NOT a "collect more versions" standard.** You already have:
- Comprehensive coverage (composer, standards era, contemporary)
- Multiple approaches (solo, trio, big band, vocal, remix)
- Chronological range (1940s-2024)

**Only acquire additional versions if:**
1. **Miles' Round About Midnight** - This is the big gap, the definitive studio version
2. **Betty Carter vocal** - If you want another vocal approach beyond Vaughan
3. **Versions appear on albums you're acquiring for other reasons** - Don't chase 'Round Midnight specifically

**The value here is comparative listening**, not completism.
