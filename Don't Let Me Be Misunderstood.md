## Notes

Research sources: Stereogum's list of 21 covers (the targets, the 1964 Simone date, and the three composers) and my memory (album titles marked "verify"). Wikipedia's page for this song could not be fetched. Stereogum ranks reviews by themed awards, not by quality, and its reviewer is often unkind to the covers, so the Proposed column reflects fit with your five-readings test more than the reviewer's taste.

Stereogum also lists readings I left out: Eric Burdon's live beach reggae version, Eric Burdon with Jenny Lewis, John Legend, The Killers with Andrew Stockdale, Brent Smith of Shinedown, Chris Sedgwick with Ozzy Osbourne, Zeds Dead, and Red Band. The reviewer found each of them dull, derivative, or a novelty.
