## Notes

Research note: every target comes from the Wikipedia article "Sinner Man", and I have not checked a second source. Nina Simone's 1965 recording is the best-known version. The song descends from African American spirituals; the lyrics resemble "On the Judgement Day" and "No Hiding Place Down Here" (Old South Quartette, 1928). The Wailers also recorded the song at Studio One in 1966, with Peter Tosh and Bunny Wailer on lead vocals. I did not find an album for that recording, so it is not a target yet. Pete Seeger performed the song at Mandel Hall in 1957, but no release is identified. Simone's version has been sampled and quoted by Kanye West, Timbaland, Felix da Housecat, Abd al-Malik, Hozier with Mavis Staples ("Nina Cried Power"), and Celeste. The Talib Kweli target is the only sample on this page. The Goose version exists only as live performances.

Correction: earlier in this conversation I said the Weavers recorded the song in the early 1950s. Wikipedia dates the Weavers version to 1959.
