Ranked shortlist of 12 blues songs, drafted 2026-11-01 from John's list of 11 plus gap songs. First-recording data comes from Wikipedia's "List of blues standards". The ranking is a proposal and is open to change.

## Ranking criteria

1. The song has many distinct, well-regarded versions, because a versions page needs material.
2. The list holds no more than one song per source lineage, unless the versions differ strongly.
3. The list spans eras and regions: pre-war women, Delta, Texas, Chicago, and jazz-adjacent.
4. The song links to existing jazz pages or to records already in the collection.

## Cut by John

- *Little Wing*: psychedelic soul, not blues.
- *How Blue Can You Get*: B.B. King has owned the song since 1964, and few other versions exist.
- *Walkin' Blues*: it overlaps *Cross Road Blues* in the Robert Johnson and Son House lineage.

## Reserve list

- *Come On in My Kitchen*: a third Robert Johnson song, so it overlaps ranks 5 and 6.
- *Stagger Lee* and *House of the Rising Sun*: folk ballads with huge version counts. They suit a separate "ballads" group more than a blues group.
- *Sweet Home Chicago*, *Key to the Highway*, *Hoochie Coochie Man*: strong Chicago and Delta candidates if any slot opens.
