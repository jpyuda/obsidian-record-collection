Ranked shortlist of 12 songs (10 at the first draft; *Go Tell It on the Mountain* and *Sinnerman* were added later) for the gospel group, drafted 2026-11-01. The group is a catch-all that spans four categories: spirituals, hymns, gospel, and freedom songs. The ranking is a proposal and is open to change. I did not verify first-recording dates, so the Origin column lists only what I am confident about.

## Ranking criteria

1. The song has many distinct, well-regarded versions.
2. The list covers all four categories.
3. The song links to existing pages or to records already in the collection.
4. No single artist owns the song, so the page holds a range of versions.

## Cut by John

- Cut on 2026-11-17, because the versions vary too little: *A Change Is Gonna Come*. Their pages sit in the `Archive` folder with their holdings and notes.
- Cut on 2026-11-17, after the review in `Songs that fit the model.md`: *Amazing Grace*, *How I Got Over*, *Nobody Knows the Trouble I've Seen*, and *Precious Lord, Take My Hand*. Their pages sit in the `Archive` folder with their holdings and notes.

- *Compared to What*: a Vietnam-era protest song with no gospel lineage. John decided on 2026-11-04 to keep the gospel group at 11 songs at that time and park the song for a future soul and R&B group; its page remains as a stub.

- *We Shall Overcome*: few distinct versions beyond the movement recordings.
- *Oh Happy Day*: most versions are pop covers of the Edwin Hawkins Singers' 1969 recording.

## Links to existing jazz pages

- *When the Saints Go Marchin' In* and *Come Sunday*: link to the jazz pages. A gospel page would hold only a stub.

## Reserve list

- *Strange Fruit*: Billie Holiday and Nina Simone; a protest song, not a freedom song in the strict sense. It is the first addition if the freedom-song count needs to grow.
- *Oh Happy Day* and *We Shall Overcome*: cut for the reasons above.
- *Mississippi Goddam*: Nina Simone's song has few versions beyond hers and Dee Dee Bridgewater's live version.
- *Gotta Serve Somebody*: Dylan owns the song and few other versions exist.
- *You Gotta Reap What You Sow*: I could not verify its versions.
