Assessment of which blues, gospel, and soul songs fit the standards model, written 2026-11-17. The test: does the song have at least five readings in different idioms that you want to hear? A song that fails the test becomes a note about one definitive recording, and album and artist pages serve that purpose better.

## How I judged

I counted the idioms that the holdings and targets on each page span, and I added what I know about the song's wider tradition. The vault's target lists came mostly from Wikipedia and SecondHandSongs, so they may under-count idioms for songs that few researchers have documented. Treat each rating as a judgment, not a measurement.

These fail the test for soul: Respect, I Heard It Through the Grapevine, Stand By Me (no holdings), In the Midnight Hour, and Yes We Can Can. The versions of these songs mostly follow one definitive arrangement.

## Suggested cuts

- **Drop or demote:** Red House, How I Got Over, and Lift Every Voice and Sing (borderline). Keep them as pages only if you value the versions you already own.
- **Soul shortlist:** Compared to What, I Put a Spell on You, Don't Let Me Be Misunderstood, People Get Ready, Night Time Is the Right Time, and Drown in My Own Tears. Add Feeling Good to the jazz list.
