Candidate list for the future soul and R&B group, drafted 2026-11-04 and revised after your holdings list. It is a list to choose from, and no song is final. Pick five or six targets.

## How I built the list

The plan was to rank candidates by the number of cover versions on SecondHandSongs. The test did not work. The site rejected 11 of my 12 page requests, and the one page that loaded (People Get Ready) gave no total. The ranking below therefore rests on my judgment and on your holdings. Treat every claim about cover counts as unverified.

Each holding below was checked against a Discogs tracklist in your collection. Recording dates are not yet added.

## Items to check

- The Hooker credit on the Folk Blues Festival set comes from your note. The Discogs tracklist for that release names no performer for B4.
- The Marley holding is a medley. It may not count as a full version of People Get Ready.
- I checked only some of your Ray Charles albums. *The Great Ray Charles*, *The Genius of Ray Charles*, *Yes Indeed!*, and *Modern Sounds in Country and Western Music* may hold more of these songs.
- Your Aretha *Atlantic Singles (1968)* box was not checked. It may hold more of these songs.
- The Night Time Is the Right Time origin (Roosevelt Sykes, 1937) comes from my memory and needs a source.

## Probably cut

- *(Sittin' On) The Dock of the Bay*: Otis Redding owns the song, and you own his album.
- *Hit the Road Jack*: Ray Charles owns the song, with few notable covers.
