Candidate list for the future soul and R&B group, drafted 2026-11-04. It is a list to choose from, and no song is final. Pick five or six targets.

## How I built the list

The plan was to rank candidates by the number of cover versions on SecondHandSongs. The test did not work. The site rejected 11 of my 12 page requests, and the one page that loaded (People Get Ready) gave no total. The ranking below therefore rests on my judgment. The only evidence is the artists who appeared in search results for each song. Treat every claim as unverified until you check it.

## Candidates

## Probably cut

- *Respect*: Otis Redding's song, and Aretha Franklin owns the famous version. Few other significant versions exist. You own Otis Redding's *Live in Europe*, which opens with it.
- *(Sittin' On) The Dock of the Bay*: Otis Redding owns the song, and you own his album.
- *Hit the Road Jack*: Ray Charles owns the song, with few notable covers.

## My suggested six

1. People Get Ready
2. Stand By Me
3. Ain't No Sunshine
4. What'd I Say
5. Mercy, Mercy, Mercy
6. Compared to What

Swap in *Drown in My Own Tears* or *The "In" Crowd* if one of the six has too few versions you can find.
