## Items to check

- The Hooker credit on the Folk Blues Festival set comes from your note. The Discogs tracklist for that release names no performer for B4.
- The Marley holding is a medley. It may not count as a full version of People Get Ready.
- I checked only some of your Ray Charles albums. *The Great Ray Charles*, *The Genius of Ray Charles*, *Yes Indeed!*, and *Modern Sounds in Country and Western Music* may hold more of these songs.
- Your Aretha *Atlantic Singles (1968)* box was not checked. It may hold more of these songs.
- The Night Time Is the Right Time origin (Roosevelt Sykes, 1937) comes from my memory and needs a source.
