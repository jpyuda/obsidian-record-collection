# My Funny Valentine

## Personal Note
**My favorite ballad.** This isn't just documentation - this is a love affair with one composition.

## Composition Info
- Composer: Richard Rodgers (music), Lorenz Hart (lyrics), 1937
- From the musical *Babes in Arms*
- Originally sung by character who thinks her valentine isn't conventionally attractive but loves them anyway
- Hart's self-deprecating, vulnerable lyric became THE jazz ballad
- Ted Gioia: "One of the most recorded songs in jazz history" (p. XXX)

## The Chet Baker Deep Dive Option

Chet recorded this constantly across his entire career (1950s-1980s). If you want to track **one artist's lifetime relationship** with this song (like Jamal/Poinciana), Chet is the candidate.
